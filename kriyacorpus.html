Access & Offering Notice

Every recording below carries a short preview clip (10 seconds or less) so you can feel the quality of the recording — the room, the silence, the breath — before going further.

The full-length recording is made available on receipt of a small pranami — a devotional offering, not a commercial fee. In the Kriya Yoga lineage, such offerings accompanying the sharing of teaching go back to the time of Lahiri Mahasaya and, in tradition, to Mahavatar Babaji himself; they sustain the guru-disciple current rather than monetize it.

  1. Preview — watch the short clip embedded on this page, freely, any time.
  2. Offer — send $3 USD per recording using the button below each entry.
  3. Receive — email your payment reference and the recording's title; the full-length share link is sent to you directly, usually within 24 hours.

We do not publish full-length links on this page — only the person who has made the offering receives them, kept safe and personal, the way a teaching is traditionally received directly from hand to hand.

Charter

Vision & Mission

Vision

The REAL Kriya Corpus preserves recorded silence, breath, and technique so that the highest teachings of Gurudev Paramahamsa Hariharananda Giri remain available — not as content, but as a living transmission — to sincere seekers wherever they are.

Mission

The Corpus records group and solitary sittings in deep meditation and silence, pranayama and breath-control practice, short satsangs and practice sharings on technique, tips, and insight, and — as they are prepared — direct instructional recordings on the technique itself. Recordings are catalogued, previewed briefly for anyone, and released in full to those who make a small pranami offering, supporting the ongoing research, recording, and teaching mission of the REAL Institute. Corpus is growing; new sittings are added as they are recorded.
