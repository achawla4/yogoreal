Meet the Masters

The self-realized spiritual masters of the Kriya Yoga lineage — guiding householders and monastics alike into the science of breath, devotion, and divine stillness.

Kriya Yoga is an ancient, unbroken spiritual science passed down through direct initiation from teacher to student. The lineage integrates practical householder life with profound monastic contemplation, teaching that every human being carries equal potential for self-realization.

Master 01

Mahavatar Babaji

The Eternal Deathless Master

The timeless Himalayan master who revived the ancient science of Kriya Yoga in modern times. In 1861, near Ranikhet in the Himalayas, Babaji initiated Shyama Charan Lahiri Mahasaya, restoring Kriya Yoga to humanity as a universal technique for liberation.

"Kriya Yoga is an instrument through which human evolution can be quickened. The law of cause and effect operate in both outer nature and inner consciousness."
Master 02

Shyama Charan Lahiri Mahasaya

Yogiraj (1828 – 1895) — The Householder Master

A devout accountant, family man, and spiritual titan residing in Varanasi. Lahiri Mahasaya demonstrated that the highest states of Samadhi and self-realization are fully attainable while fulfilling household, professional, and family duties.

"Banat Banat Ban Jai" — Always practicing, practicing, one day it will be done. Solve all your problems through meditation.
Master 03

Swami Sri Yukteswar Giri

Jnanavatar (1855 – 1936) — Incarnation of Wisdom

A master of astronomy, Vedic astrology, and scriptural commentary. Author of The Holy Science (Kaivalya Darsanam), Sri Yukteswarji established ashrams in Serampore and Puri, trainingParamahansa Yogananda and Paramahamsa Hariharananda in strict discipline and deep scriptural unity.

"Wisdom is the greatest cleanser. Good intent without discipline is incomplete."
Master 04

Paramahansa Yogananda

Messenger of Kriya Yoga to the World (1893 – 1952)

Author of the world-renowned Autobiography of a Yogi. Sent to the West by Mahavatar Babaji and Sri Yukteswarji in 1920, Yoganandaji introduced millions to Kriya Yoga, scientific meditation, and Self-Realization Fellowship worldwide.

"Self-realization is the knowing in all parts of body, mind, and soul that you are now in possession of the kingdom of God."
Master 05

Paramahamsa Hariharananda Giri

Gurudev (1907 – 2002) — Master of Divine Love & Breath

Inspiration and spiritual guide of the Hariharananda REAL Institute. Attained Nirvikalpa Samadhi after mastering all higher stages of Kriya under Sri Yukteswarji, Yoganandaji, and Sannyal Mahasaya. He traveled tirelessly across India, Europe, and the Americas, bringing breath-awareness and meditation to all.

"Breath is God. Every breath is your divine life. With every breath, feel the presence of the Almighty within."
Master 06

Swami Satyananda Giri

Master of Devotion & Education (1896 – 1971)

Direct disciple of Sri Yukteswarji and close boyhood companion of Paramahansa Yogananda. Swami Satyanandaji served as leader of Karar Ashram in Puri and established educational and spiritual institutions in Sevayatan and Malkha Nagar.

"True education is the unfolding of the soul's divine potential through service and meditation."
Master 07

Bhupendranath Sannyal Mahasaya

The Ideal Householder Master (1877 – 1962)

Initiated by Lahiri Mahasaya at age 16. Sannyal Mahasaya taught alongside Rabindranath Tagore at Santiniketan, wrote extensive commentaries on the Bhagavad Gita, and established Gurudham Ashrams in Puri and Bhagalpur.

"Live in the world as a lotus flower lives in water — untouched by mud, fully dedicated to divine light."
